# Blog draft — Why I keep plain-text notes

*Draft — don't share yet.*

I have tried every notes app. The ones that lasted were the ones that stored my notes as plain Markdown files in a folder I own.

Three reasons:

1. **They outlive the app.** A folder of `.md` files opens in anything, today and in twenty years.
2. **They are greppable.** The fastest search is the one you don't have to think about.
3. **Links are just text.** `[[Another note]]` is readable even without the app.

What I want from the app on top: instant search, quick capture, and a way to see what connects to what — without a database I can't read.
