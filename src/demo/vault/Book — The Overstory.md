# Book — The Overstory

Richard Powers. About halfway through.

Nine people whose lives are tied to trees. The first part is a set of short stories; the second braids them together. Slow, but I keep thinking about it on walks.

Favourite line so far: "The best time to plant a tree was twenty years ago. The second-best time is now."

Want to read it again on the train in Lisbon. On the [[Reading list]].
