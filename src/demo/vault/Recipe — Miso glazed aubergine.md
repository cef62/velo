# Recipe — Miso glazed aubergine

Serves 2 as a main.

- 2 aubergines, halved, flesh scored in a diamond pattern
- 2 tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp sesame oil

1. Roast the aubergines cut-side down at 200 °C for 25 minutes.
2. Mix the glaze, brush it on, and grill for 5 minutes until it bubbles.
3. Top with sesame seeds and spring onion. Serve with rice.

Made this for the team dinner — everyone asked for the recipe.
