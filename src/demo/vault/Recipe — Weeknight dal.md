# Recipe — Weeknight dal

Serves 4.

## Ingredients

- 250 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, a thumb of ginger
- 1 tsp each: cumin seeds, turmeric, garam masala
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tin coconut milk
- Lime and coriander to finish

## Method

1. Fry the cumin seeds in oil until they pop.
2. Add onion, garlic and ginger; cook until soft.
3. Stir in the spices, then lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk and 500 ml water.
4. Simmer 20 minutes, stirring now and then.
5. Finish with lime juice and coriander.
